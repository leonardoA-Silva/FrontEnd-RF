# Reaproveita Franca

## Overview
Editorial circular-economy identity, warm paper and forest green. Existing account routes preserve their API contracts. The selected cinematic home uses explicitly approved GSAP, Three.js, React Three Fiber and Drei.

## Layout
Asymmetric home, independent compact login, guided registration workspace and forest dashboard sidebar. Fluid grids stack on phones. Existing Tailwind styles objects and MUI forms remain.

## Elevation & Depth
Generated, locally hosted WebP cutouts reproduce the material-led composition. GSAP animates scoped reveals and scroll parallax, with cleanup on navigation and reduced-motion support. A pause control disables decorative motion. An explicit “Explorar em 3D” action lazy-loads a procedural, simplified ribbon sculpture (not a GLB replica of the illustration), with pointer/keyboard controls and fallback. Demand rendering stops automatic motion offscreen or in hidden tabs. Other account pages use restrained transitions.

## Components
Forest primary actions, visible focus, native account radio selection, readable form surfaces. Dashboard data is explicitly identified as demonstration. No claims of live reservations, impact, maps or AI.

## Verification
Lint, production build and responsive route inspection. No real personal data or credentials in captures.


## Account flows
Login is an independent access composition: full-height forest background, compact ivory form card, inverse brand, simple back link and minimal footer, with no marketing sculpture or shared account hero. Profile selection is a spacious editorial page with two photographic choices. Registration is a practical light workspace with a vertical progress rail, neutral page heading and numbered form sheet. On phones, the rail becomes a short horizontal progress row. Shared field styling, brand tokens and domain logic remain consistent; layouts are distinct. Existing API contracts, OTP behavior and validation remain unchanged. No new dependency or dashboard changes are part of this refinement.


## Company workspace
The selected company concept uses a narrow forest rail, ivory workspace, serif editorial headings, compact inline totals, fine table rules, natural material imagery and a modest guidance strip. Nested pages cover materials, publication, editing, details, account and delete confirmation. Forms are practical and visually consistent, not copies of the marketing home. Preview is interactive and explicitly in-memory; production is read-only for product mutation until confirmed server-side company/ownership checks. Account editing uses the verified protected own-user endpoint.
